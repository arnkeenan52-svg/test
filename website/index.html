Good questions.

Does it really build inside Studio?

Yes. Forge installs as a native dock widget and creates the actual scripts, UI, and terrain in the correct services — with full undo.

How is this different from a code chatbot?

A chatbot hands you a snippet to paste. Forge places working, server-safe systems straight into your game, builds the world around them, and learns from your playtests.

Is the code safe to ship?

Best practices are on by default: the server never trusts the client, remotes are validated, and saving is wrapped with retries and shutdown saves.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — monthly, cancel whenever. Free stays free.
