Good questions.

Does it really build inside Roblox Studio?

Yes. Forge installs as a native dock widget and creates the actual Script, LocalScript, ModuleScript, GUI, and terrain instances in the correct services — with full undo.

How is this different from a code chatbot?

Chatbots hand you a snippet to paste. Forge places working, server-authoritative systems directly into your game, builds the world around them, and learns from your playtests to optimize.

Is the generated code safe to ship?

Best practices are on by default: the server never trusts the client, remotes are validated, DataStores are pcall-wrapped with retries and BindToClose.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — monthly, cancel whenever. Free stays free forever.
